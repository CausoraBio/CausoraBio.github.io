01 / APPROACH

01 / OUR APPROACH

Start with the biology. Follow the evidence.

We use AI to interrogate complex biological data and formulate hypotheses that can be tested experimentally. Our emphasis is on mechanistic interpretation, critical appraisal of evidence, and iterative learning from experimental results.

01

Integrate complementary evidence

Genetic, multi-omic, and published evidence each capture distinct aspects of disease biology. Their integration supports a more coherent account of disease-associated processes.

02

Interrogate disease causality

Association provides a starting point. We examine whether an observed alteration contributes to disease pathogenesis and whether its modulation may have therapeutic relevance.

03

Subject hypotheses to experiment

A scientifically useful hypothesis should yield a discriminating experiment. Conclusions remain provisional and are refined as additional evidence becomes available.

02 / RESEARCH PROCESS

02 / HOW WE PLAN TO WORK

From characterizing disease to selecting decisive experiments.

01

Define a focused question

Specify the disease question, relevant patient population, and biological system under investigation.

02

Assemble and appraise evidence

Integrate relevant datasets and literature while evaluating methodological quality, uncertainty, and discordant findings.

03

Prioritize candidate targets

Assess mechanistic rationale, tractability, potential modes of intervention, and translational constraints.

04

Test, update, and refine

Design experiments that discriminate among hypotheses, then use the results to update the next research question.

This framework is under active development. Any proposed target requires expert scientific review and experimental validation before its therapeutic relevance can be assessed.

03 / RESEARCH INTERESTS

03 / RESEARCH INTERESTS

Questions we aim to investigate.

Our interests encompass disease biology, therapeutic target discovery, and the development of AI systems that support rigorous and reproducible biomedical research.

Disease mechanisms

Which alterations in genes, proteins, and cellular states contribute to disease, and through which interactions do their effects emerge?

Therapeutic targets

Which components of a disease mechanism are amenable to intervention, and what evidentiary standard should support their prioritization?

AI research agents

How can AI support evidence review, mechanistic reasoning, and experimental planning while preserving transparency and scientific scrutiny?

04 / TOOLS

04 / TOOLS

Three complementary systems for biomedical research.

We are developing an integrated suite of research tools to organize evidence, investigate disease mechanisms, and structure subsequent analytical and experimental work.

TARGET DISCOVERY

CausoraBio Discovery

Investigates disease biology and candidate targets by integrating genetic and multi-omic evidence, comparing mechanistic hypotheses, and supporting validation planning.

Planned focus: mechanism analysis, target assessment, and validation planning.
BIOMEDICAL EVIDENCE

CausoraBio Atlas

Maps relationships among genes, proteins, pathways, diseases, and published findings while retaining the sources and context required for critical evaluation.

Planned focus: evidence retrieval, relationship mapping, and source traceability.
RESEARCH WORKFLOWS

CausoraBio Agent

Transforms a research question into a structured workflow for literature review, analytical tool coordination, and evidence-based reporting, with human review at consequential decisions.

Planned focus: task planning, tool coordination, and research reporting.

These systems are in development. Specific capabilities and availability will be reported as the research program progresses.
